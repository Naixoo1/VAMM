# VAMM demo walkthrough

This guide covers the compatible teammate features adapted into the existing VAMM Next.js app. Use the same browser profile and local address throughout: learning progress, saved research and simulation history are local to that browser, not synced accounts.

## Suggested recording sequence

1. Open **Home**. Show the VAMM AI logo, final midnight navigation and decorative learning orbit, feature cards, and real lesson/XP counts. The starting-holdings and three-turn cards describe the frozen simulation, not a live brokerage balance.
2. Open **Learning Academy**. Expand a course outline, search for “inflation”, then clear the search. The first lesson of each course is available.
3. Open **Introduction to Stocks**. Show the learning goals, labeled illustration, explanation, reflection reveal and publisher source links. Mark it complete and use **Next lesson**. Complete the second lesson to reach 20 Academy points and the first milestone. Reviewing a completed lesson awards no extra points.
4. Open **Missions & Challenges**. Choose **Stock Basics** and **Start quiz**. Questions are reviewed practice, not generated by a live AI call. There is no timer.
5. Answer a question, go forward, then use a question number to revisit it. Select **Exit quiz → Save and exit**, reload, and continue the saved draft. Choices are saved when moving between questions or explicitly saving; the page labels unsaved choices.
6. Complete all five answers and submit. Show the score, concept review and answer explanations. Four correct answers meet the 80% target. A pack awards 100 learning XP on its first saved pass only; replaying does not duplicate rewards.
7. Open **Learning Profile**. Show completed lessons, milestone badges, latest/best quiz scores and saved draft status. This is a local learning overview, not registration, authentication or a financial ranking.
8. Open **Market**. Show the official dark TradingView Advanced Chart, default NASDAQ:AAPL and daily interval. Change symbols using the provider UI. Internet access, provider availability and quote delays apply.
9. Open **Compare**. Enter exchange-qualified listings such as NASDAQ:AAPL and NASDAQ:MSFT, then select **Compare listings**. Optional suggestions are not an allowlist; the provider determines coverage. Do not claim every exchange is supported or every quote is real time.
10. Open **News & Sentiment Lab**. Show actual publisher headlines and publication times, hide/show or refresh the live feed, and follow **Practice facts vs. opinions** into its reviewed quiz. VAMM does not classify the live headlines or invent live sentiment scores.
11. Open **AI Investment Mentor**. Show the separate live company context, then expand **Synthetic basket mentor**. Choose one or two teaching baskets and ask a question. Show the actual **Model-selected explanation** or **Prewritten fallback** label, grounding and limitations. Save the completed answer, edit the form, reopen the saved snapshot and demonstrate confirmed deletion. Saved answers are independent question/answer snapshots, not conversational context or live company research.
12. Open **Simulation**. Start from zero, preview contributions, advance one year and explicitly fund the shock. Complete the existing three annual turns. A new session preserves previous history. The lesson/quiz/profile additions do not change these calculations.
13. Demonstrate **Pause animations** and **Static motion**, then a narrow viewport with its labeled, keyboard-accessible workspace menu. Motion is decorative; it never drives calculations or introduces artificial waiting.

## Honest demo labels

- Reviewed lessons/questions, hypothetical illustrations and learning rewards stay distinct from external live charts/news and the deterministic simulator.
- Market providers and publisher links require internet access. A successful local test is not a guarantee of provider uptime or universal exchange coverage.
- The QA production server disables real model calls. Its Research fallback is intentional. A normal start can use existing private server configuration; no credential belongs in browser code or a public template. The previously exposed upstream key should be revoked/rotated by its owner.
- Paid quiz generation, paid web search, full conversational chat, stock order execution and screenshot buy/sell advice are not implemented or represented as working features.
- No deployment was performed. Vercel deployment requires the user's acceptance of this build.
